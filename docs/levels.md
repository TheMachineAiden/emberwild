# Chapter progression

## Glödvik → Sävstigen

Start in village with three equally viable starter options. Bo points east and explains the free healer and capsules. The broad path exits on the east edge; houses remain obstacles. Sävstigen has optional grass patches beside the path, an early capture opportunity and Liv near its far end. Liv explains that creatures are carrying light toward the ridge. Win her battle to receive a copper token and open the eastern passage. A capture is encouraged, but never required to unlock the route.

Teaching: neutral versus typed attacks, weakening before capture, switching, free recovery. The healer's landmark remains accessible from every stage. Losing preserves all discoveries and gates.

## Vindryggen

West entry faces a curved trail with grass on both sides and the lighthouse visible to the northeast. Tide and ember species join the encounter pool, supporting every starter. A resting place provides free healing and minimum retry supplies so the lighthouse does not require a long walk after each fight. A lookout explains that the beacon was deliberately shuttered. Trainer Edda tests two different types; winning earns keeper trust and opens the cellar door. Do not demand a particular caught species.

## Lighthouse cellar

A compact room with three ember bowls, a sealed stairway and a caretaker note: “Från havet växer mossan. Ur mossan stiger glöden.” Interact with bowls in tide→moss→ember order. Show each lit bowl and reset automatically with clear feedback after a wrong choice. A reset lever is always reachable. This puzzle combines the type vocabulary without consuming items, damaging the team or requiring a specific team. Solving opens stairs persistently. A reading point shows creatures sheltering the light; this discovery must be on the required path.

## Summit and ending

Keeper Signe fears the wandering embers will burn out. Her mixed team uses two conventional creatures and Ljuskrön; all starters retain viable neutral attacks and access to healing. Entry offers a clear challenge prompt and free preparation. A failed attempt returns the healed team to the village with all gates/puzzle progress intact. Victory sets the chapter-complete flag, lights the beacon, changes the summit and village dialogue, and provides a short ending panel with return-to-world control. Keep the completed world explorable and saveable. The light is shared voluntarily by creatures; the solution pays off the mystery rather than merely awarding a badge.

## Worked dependency sequence

Starter chosen → route capture optional → Liv victory persists → ridge exit open → Edda victory persists → cellar accessible → all three bowls correctly lit → stairs open → keeper victory → beacon lit and ending available. Walking away before winning a battle or completing the bowl sequence does not falsely set the next flag. Returning after loss retains only already-earned flags. Wrong bowl input resets only the current sequence. Chapter completion does not remove healing or save controls.

Session length remains a 20–30 minute design target pending timed ordinary play. Adjust encounter density, trainer teams and dialogue based on measured experience; avoid padding travel or forcing grind to meet a duration claim.

## Ember rescue revision

Three fixed encounters provide discoverable evidence: northern reed bed and southern shore on Sävstigen, cliff shelf on Vindryggen. Capture or defeat grants the field observation exactly once; flee/loss leaves it available and no particular species is required. The field journal lives in the team menu. These observations reward optional exploration; Edda’s trust opens the cellar. Completed legacy saves retain free access. These encounters add original evidence and collection opportunities before the cellar revelation; duration still needs measurement.
