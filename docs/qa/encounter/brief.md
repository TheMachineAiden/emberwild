## Musical construction

The canonical score remains music/scores.js. These are hardware-inspired three-voice pieces: square lead/answer, triangle bass, modern browser envelopes/mixing, not strict hardware execution. Lead/bass/answer gain levels remain .035/.055/.018; the stronger arrangement does not increase level. The lead warning and main material are one composition on one continuously scheduled bus. The three-beat opening is explicit; the loop marker is after it.

Battle opens with repeated E5 over an E pedal, then descends D5–C5–B4–G-sharp4. The main A4/A2 entry resolves that dominant while presenting the established A–C–D–E ember cell in a long-short rhythm. Bars 1–2 give one-beat lead breaths to the E4/G4 answer. Bar 3 develops the cell toward A5; bar 4 descends back to C5. F in bar 5 opens the register/harmony before bar 6 answers upward. Bar 7 reaches B5 over G (a bright third), then recovers through A5. Bar 8 descends G5–E5–D5–B4–G-sharp4 and rests for half a beat before the repeated A entry. Bass uses root/fifth/octave motion with B→A and E→F approaches, without filling every lead rest.

Boss keeps the warning's repeated E but adds F5→E5 tension and a more active octave bass. Its first phrase reaches A5 immediately; bar 3 contracts toward A4/C5, bar 4 adds G-sharp5 over E/B, preparing A5 in bar 5. Bar 7 shares the B5 peak and both pieces use the same dominant return. The distinguishable bass/register/phrase placement makes boss broader and more insistent without adding a fourth voice or changing non-battle cues.

Boundary inspection covers the actual opening→main (beat 3), loop 1→2 (35), and loop 2→3 (67): outgoing G-sharp4 leads to A4; E/B bass gives way to A2. There is no overlapping occupied span in any part, including at these joins. Each envelope releases to zero 25ms before the occupied end, with short notes still longer than the 40ms envelope tail. Answers mostly occupy authored lead breaths rather than masking the high arrivals. This is an event-based rationale, not a claim about perceived musical quality.

## Audio exports and observations

battle-intro-two-loops.wav and boss-intro-two-loops.wav were rendered by Chrome OfflineAudioContext using src/audio.js's shared scheduleEvents function, the exact canonical score, live oscillator shapes/envelopes/levels and initial bus fade. scripts/export-music-browser.js produces the base64 WAV/metrics JSON through the supported browser evaluation output-file facility; decode each wav field to reproduce the retained files. audio-export.json records all source/WAV hashes and expanded events. These review files are excluded from the runtime build inventory.

Battle: 26.9s, mono PCM16/44.1kHz, float peak .10410 and RMS .03940. Boss: 25.225s, peak .10584 and RMS .03896. No clipping is measured. These native offline renders establish synthesis and export, not hearing, real-time playback quality or physical-device output. No listening verdict is claimed. Focused audition remains necessary for warning/main continuity, balance, harshness and two-loop fatigue.
