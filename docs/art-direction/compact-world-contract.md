# Compact coastal people and places

The A column in human-options-2026-11-08.png is the visual reference. The other
columns are comparison studies, not production directions. The board is a concept,
not an animation atlas. Runtime pixels are authored separately at their intended
size, with retained sources and deterministic PNG exports.

People share a compact, expressive head/body proportion, a flat human profile,
dark blue-green outlines, clear hands and separated legs. The explorer has swept
dark hair, teal/cream clothing and a russet backpack. Hair, complexion, outfit and
role accessories distinguish the cast. A projecting profile must not read as a
bill. Directional heads and feet remain anchored during the gait.

The generated A character is the human raster production source. The earlier
16×24 matrix approximation is retained history, not the active hero/Mira/Perrin
source. Preserve the selected face, large head and short body through extraction
and matching generated pose variants. The production people use24×30 logical
frames, drawn at tile offset(0,-6); its foot remains at the existing tile bottom24.
The24×24 collision grid, camera scale and UI stay unchanged. Do not paste a whole
concept scene or force a different face into a legacy frame. Generated master PNGs,
recorded crops and deterministic native exports establish the human mapping.

The environment uses the same light from the upper left, restrained deep outlines,
warm stone/plaster and russet wood, clustered cool foliage and readable pale paths.
House doors remain at their actual approach tiles. Trees retain their collision
feet and may overlap visually above the ground; water boundaries remain visibly
impassable. Keep the meadow, coast and lighthouse identities and accepted battle
composition. Creatures, UI, music, effects and gameplay are outside this art change.

First review the labelled Briarwick environment concept as a style hypothesis,
before detailed native production. It does not establish topology, sprite sizes,
gameplay or animation quality. The existing map and collision data remain the
implementation authority. Then make the playable sample: Briarwick explorer,
Mira and Perrin, one house/door, trees,
grass/path and water edge at844×390 and667×375. Retain matched native captures,
directional idle/walk observations and source-to-PNG mappings before scaling the
cast/environment set. Existing movement, controls, trainer and PWA tests protect
integration; art approval requires rendered contact, occlusion and motion review.
