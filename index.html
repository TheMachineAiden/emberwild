Field guide

Ember beats Moss. Moss beats Tide. Tide beats Ember. Move cards show the damage for the current matchup.

In wild battles, choose Catch to use a Spark Vessel. Weaken the creature first for a better chance. A Mending Draught restores up to 22 HP. Mira and the camps heal your whole team and refill supplies for free.

Arrows or WASD walk. Hold Shift or the running icon to sprint. Enter or the speech icon talks or inspects. Escape opens your team while exploring and goes back in battle menus. Tab or arrow keys move between commands.
