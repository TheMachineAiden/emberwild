Field guide

Ember beats Moss. Moss beats Tide. Tide beats Ember. Move cards show the damage for the current matchup.

Weaken wild creatures before using a Spark Vessel. A Mending Draught restores up to 22 HP. Mira and the camps heal your whole team and refill supplies for free.

Arrows or WASD walk. Hold Shift or Sprint to run. Enter talks or inspects. Escape opens your team while exploring and goes back in battle menus. Tab or arrow keys move between commands.
