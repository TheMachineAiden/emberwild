# Emberwild music

Canonical event scores live in `scores.js`, independently of the Web Audio renderer in `src/audio.js`. Timing uses quarter-note beats, C4=MIDI 60, explicit occupied durations and R rests; bars are four beats. Gaps in a part are authored rests. Five 8-bar loops and two 1-bar motifs use pulse-like square lead/answer and triangle bass. This is hardware-inspired, with modern browser oscillators, envelopes and mixing; it does not claim strict chip execution. Noise is reserved for later SFX.

The ember cell A4 (long), C5 (short), D5 (quarter), breath opens the village cue. Route compresses the first two notes and extends the answer upward to A5. Battle fragments the cell into half-beat attacks; boss broadens the arrival to A5 and introduces a B5 peak in bar 7. Landmark slows the cell, holds A over an A pedal, then rests before the E bass turn. Victory resolves the cell to A5; capture retains its long-short pickup.

Score review: village bar 4 leaves a lead rest for the descending C5–B4–A4 answer; bar 8 leaves space after G4 as the bass E2 prepares return to A2. Route bar 8 descends E5–D5–B4 and leaves a quarter rest before the initial A4. Battle bar 8 ends on A4 with a rest, allowing bass E3–G2 to lead into A2. Boss bar 7 B5 over G creates a bright third before A5; bar 8 drops to B4 and rests over E to restart the A center. Landmark bar 8 holds A4 against E2 as an intentionally unresolved fifth before the A2 return. No channel has overlapping occupied spans. All releases reach zero 0.025 seconds before the occupied end.

Playback: explicit Sound button gesture creates/resumes the context; default is muted. Changing cues stops all scheduled voices and starts the new cue at its first beat. This is a deliberate reset transition rather than a claim that arbitrary harmonic exits connect seamlessly. Victory/capture play once until Continue changes the game state. Re-rendering the same scene never retriggers a cue. Mute and hidden-page transitions stop every scheduled oscillator and clear the loop timer; returning to the page restarts the selected cue only if enabled. Separate cues never share a running schedule. Critical feedback also appears as text.

Evidence: score bounds/channel checks and game-state routing tests. No listening verdict is claimed. Eventual audition should cover two consecutive loops, frequent action changes, capture/victory interruption, mute/unmute, page hiding and mobile gesture startup; perceived balance and fatigue remain open.
