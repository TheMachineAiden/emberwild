# Emberwild music

Canonical event scores live in `scores.js`, independently of the Web Audio renderer in `src/audio.js`. Timing uses quarter-note beats, C4=MIDI 60, explicit occupied durations and R rests; bars are four beats. Gaps in a part are authored rests. Three 8-bar exploration loops, two 3-beat-entry + 8-bar battle pieces and two 1-bar motifs use pulse-like square lead/answer and triangle bass. This is hardware-inspired, with modern browser oscillators, envelopes and mixing; it does not claim strict chip execution. Noise is reserved for later SFX.

The ember cell A4 (long), C5 (short), D5 (quarter), breath opens the village cue. Route compresses the first two notes and extends the answer upward to A5. Battle retains the long-short A–C pickup, compresses D into the E arrival, and answers its one-beat breaths. Boss broadens the first arrival to A5, drives a more active octave bass and shares the B5 peak in bar 7. Each encounter begins with a three-beat E-pedal warning that descends through G-sharp to the A-minor main downbeat; this is part of the same piece, not a separate sound. Landmark slows the cell, holds A over an A pedal, then rests before the E bass turn. Victory resolves the cell to A5; capture retains its long-short pickup.

Score review: village bar 4 leaves a lead rest for the descending C5–B4–A4 answer; bar 8 leaves space after G4 as the bass E2 prepares return to A2. Route bar 8 descends E5–D5–B4 and leaves a quarter rest before the initial A4. Both battle pieces approach their loop return with G-sharp4 and a half-beat breath over E/B; the main A4/A2 entry resolves that dominant. Boss bar 4 uses G-sharp5 over E/B before the A5 arrival in bar 5. Bar 7 B5 over G creates a bright third before A5. See docs/qa/encounter/brief.md for the opening, phrase and boundary review. Landmark bar 8 holds A4 against E2 as an intentionally unresolved fifth before the A2 return. No channel has overlapping occupied spans. All releases reach zero 0.025 seconds before the occupied end.

Playback: explicit Sound button gesture creates/resumes the context; default is muted. Changing cues stops all scheduled voices. A genuine world-to-battle transition starts the three-beat opening once; later same-cue renders do nothing. Muting or hiding consumes that opening: later enabling/resuming starts at the main section, never the warning. A cancelled visual entry releases the warning and enters the main section. The natural opening-to-main join and subsequent repeats retain one bus and an absolute musical clock, without a track restart. This is a deliberate reset transition rather than a claim that arbitrary harmonic exits connect seamlessly. Victory/capture play once until Continue changes the game state. Re-rendering the same scene never retriggers a cue. Mute and hidden-page transitions stop every scheduled oscillator and clear the loop timer; returning to the page restarts the selected main section only if enabled. Separate cues never share a running schedule. Critical feedback also appears as text.

Evidence: score bounds/channel checks and game-state routing tests. Exact-score Web Audio review WAVs (intro plus two main loops), event listings and source hashes are in docs/qa/encounter/. No listening verdict is claimed from those exports or event checks. Audition should cover their musical joins, perceived balance and repeated-play fatigue; integrated normal-input lifecycle and visual checks are tracked separately in that directory.
